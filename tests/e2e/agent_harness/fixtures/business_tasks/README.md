# Business task inputs

These synthetic fixtures are authored around business decisions, not around a prescribed Tool trace. `authoring.py` reproduces the CSV, business notes, requests and evaluator-only `oracle.json` files. Neither the generator nor the truth is exposed to Subject. Do not import materials from volatile task packets into the benchmark.

| Task | Standard variation | Confirmation variation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue revision | Four regions, eight stores, duplicate export rows, separate IDs with identical attributes, multiple refunds on one order, old-order refunds, cancelled orders, adjacent months, decimals and a refund-only region. Corrections flip the leading region. | Correcting the amount and date of existing refunds, adding a refund on an old order, and excluding later-month refunds keep the original leader while changing three regional totals. It is not a renamed copy with the same decision. |
| Campaign eligibility | 32 distinct customers; July policy alongside expired June policy. Threshold and contact-date boundaries, VIP's limited exemption, never contacted, open service cases, inactive customers and cities outside scope all affect selection. | August changes the spending threshold, contact interval and eligible cities. Three dated policy documents are present. The same customer identities allow observation of policy-sensitive membership changes. |
| Routing reuse | 90 independently written history messages and 30 new messages, across logistics, returns/refunds and invoices, in Chinese and English. Historic channels correlate with labels; the new batch changes that association. Customer batches may contain related records. | Another 30 authored messages include cross-topic mentions, explicit negation and a current request that differs from background context. History remains the same, so the variation tests changed business language rather than more training data. |

Each task remains one unit of coverage. Two requests, two input variations, 90 training rows or repeated live runs do not turn one routing task into many independent business successes. These fixtures have not been sampled from a measured population of Xenix users; their results cannot establish production accuracy or task frequency.

Business notes accompany the user message as text; table files are real composer attachments. Policies enter the Knowledge Library before the campaign request. Revenue corrections and new tickets are supplied only with the second request in the same conversation. Authoritative labels and monetary/customer expectations stay on the evaluator side.

Ticket identifiers use a text-derived opaque suffix and do not encode the target queue. Alternative output labels remain acceptable when the answer explains their business meaning; a correlated input column is not a prediction. Revenue tables may include correct aggregate rows, and campaign deliveries may use an explicitly flagged full audit table. Requested downloadable tables and analyzer entry points are stated in the prompts rather than hidden in the oracle.

Reproduce inputs with `.venv/Scripts/python.exe tests/e2e/agent_harness/fixtures/business_tasks/authoring.py`. Use the standard inputs for ordinary development; run `--business-variant confirmation` after implementation to check the separately authored business changes. Repeated tuning against confirmation answers would consume their role as confirmation evidence and should be recorded as such.

Oracle values are checked through independently expressed computations and explicit counterexamples in the task packet's design experiments. A reference classifier establishes that the text contains learnable signal; it is not the Agent and is never used to replace a live Subject result. Linked result tables may reorder/rename columns and add notes. The Judge receives the actual deliveries and explanatory scoring anchors, rather than a reconstructed successful Tool sequence.
