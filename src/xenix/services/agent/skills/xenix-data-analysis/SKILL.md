# Xenix Data Analysis

Match aggregation grain, joins and comparisons to the business question. Totals, rates and averages answer different questions; preserve the meaning of identifiers, missing values and time periods.

Use relevant Knowledge sources for business definitions, distinguishing their claims from computed observations. Association and correlation alone do not establish causality.

Choose a chart when it makes a pattern clearer. Word clouds need term frequencies, not raw sentences; time trends need a meaningful time scale. Domain examples and chart templates are available in the resource index.
